✨ About Shri Ranchoddas Arogya Bhavan

Nestled in the serene hills of Matheran, Shri Ranchoddas Arogya Bhavan is more than just a guest house—it’s a family tradition of hospitality. For generations, we’ve welcomed travelers seeking peace, simplicity, and a genuine connection to nature.

Our home offers clean, comfortable rooms with warm bedding and hot water during cooler months. While we keep things simple—without AC—we ensure every guest feels cared for with thoughtful amenities and heartfelt service.

🌿 Our Philosophy

We believe in living close to nature, embracing Matheran’s timeless charm, and offering hospitality rooted in tradition. Guests often tell us they feel less like visitors and more like part of our extended family.

🏞 Unique Experiences

  • Scenic views of lush valleys and trails right at your doorstep
  • Easy access to local attractions and hidden paths for exploration
  • A calm environment, perfect for relaxation, meditation, or creative inspiration

💌 A Note from Our Family
